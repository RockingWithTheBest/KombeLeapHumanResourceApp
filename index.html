What You Need to Prepare

Kombz Leap Enterprises Ltd | HR & Administration

Identity & Legal

  • NRC Number: As printed on your card.
  • Digital Copies: Clear photos of Front & Back of NRC.
  • Police Clearance: Valid certificate file (PDF/JPG).

Benefits & Security

  • NAPSA Number: Your Social Security number.
  • NHIMA Number: Your health insurance number.
  • Next of Kin: Full name and active phone number.

Employment Records

  • Signed Contract: The copy you received from HR.
  • Qualifications: List of certificates (Grade 12, Diplomas, etc.).
  • DOB: Date of birth.

Residential Address

  • Location: Where you currently live.
