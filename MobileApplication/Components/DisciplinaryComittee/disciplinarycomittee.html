Disciplinary Code & Conduct

Upholding Integrity, Fairness, and Accountability at Kombz Leap Enterprises

Our Principles

All disciplinary actions are administered in accordance with the Employment Code Act No. 3 of 2019 and guided by:

  • Fair Hearing: Right to be informed and respond to allegations.
  • Consistency: Similar offences attract similar sanctions.
  • Proportionality: Sanctions match the gravity of the offence.
  • Right to Representation: Accompanied by a fellow employee during hearings.

Zero Tolerance & Gross Misconduct

The following offences may lead to Summary Dismissal (Termination without Notice):

  • Corruption, Theft, or Fraud
  • Sexual Harassment, Abuse, or Exploitation
  • Reporting for duty under influence of alcohol/drugs
  • Absence without permission for >3 consecutive days
  • Gross negligence causing loss or sabotage
  • Assault or malicious damage to property

Note: Progressive discipline (Verbal → Written → Final Warning) applies to minor offences like lateness or uniform violations.
